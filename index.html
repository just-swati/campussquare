FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How does CampusSquare work?

Tell us your goals through the form, a call or WhatsApp. A counsellor shortlists suitable programs, compares universities on approvals, fees and outcomes, and helps you apply. You decide — we guide.

Is the counselling really free?

Yes. Counselling, comparison and application support are free for students. You pay the program fee only to the university.

Are online degrees valid for jobs and higher studies?

Yes. Under the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020, online degrees from UGC-entitled universities are treated as equivalent to degrees earned in regular mode. We only recommend programs that are entitled for the session you join.

What is the difference between online and distance learning?

Online programs usually include live and recorded lectures, a learning portal and online exams. Distance learning relies more on self-study material with fewer live sessions. We’ll help you pick the format that suits you.

Can I pay the fees in EMIs?

Many universities offer semester-wise payment and EMI plans, often through financing partners. Your counsellor will share the exact options for the universities you shortlist.

How soon will a counsellor contact me?

Usually within 24 hours of your enquiry, Monday to Saturday. For a faster reply, message us on WhatsApp.
