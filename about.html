About CampusSquare

Honest guidance for every learner’s next step

We help students and working professionals choose the right online degree, certificate or school-completion program — and we stay with them until they graduate.

Our story

Choosing a degree shouldn’t feel like guesswork

India now has hundreds of approved online programs. That is great news for learners — but it also means glossy ads, confusing fee structures and advice that isn’t always in the student’s interest.

CampusSquare exists to be the guide in between. We listen to your goals first, shortlist programs that genuinely fit, verify every approval, and explain the full cost up front. Then we help you apply and settle in.

Our counselling is free for students, and your fees always go directly to the university.

Our mission

To make quality higher education accessible to every learner in India with clear, unbiased and personal guidance — at no cost to the student.

Our vision

A future where anyone, anywhere — a working parent, a first-generation graduate, a small-town student — can earn a respected degree without leaving their life behind.

What we stand for

Four promises we keep

Student first

We recommend what fits your goals and budget — not what pays us the most.

Transparent fees

Full costs explained up front. Fees are paid directly to the university.

Verified options

Only programs with valid UGC-DEB entitlement and the right approvals.

Support beyond admission

Help with onboarding, exams and career planning until you graduate.
