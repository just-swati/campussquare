Is an online degree valid?

Yes — when it’s from a UGC-entitled university

  • Accepted for private-sector jobs and promotions
  • Valid for higher studies, including master’s programs
  • Recognised for government jobs that ask for a degree
  • We confirm entitlement for your exact program and session

What the regulation says

Under the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020, degrees earned online from entitled institutions are treated as equivalent to degrees earned in regular mode.

Always check a university’s entitlement on the UGC-DEB website before you enrol — our counsellors do this for you.

FAQs

Questions before you apply

Is the counselling really free?

Yes. Counselling, comparison and application support are free for students. You pay the program fee only to the university.

Can I study while working full-time?

Yes. Online programs are designed for working learners, with recorded lectures, weekend live sessions and online exams at most universities.

What documents will I need?

Usually your 10th and 12th mark sheets (plus graduation mark sheets for a PG program), a photo ID such as Aadhaar, and a passport-size photo. Your counsellor will share the exact list for your university.

When does the next session start?

Most universities run two intakes a year, typically around January and July. Deadlines vary, so book a call to see the current dates for your program.
